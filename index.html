Escaping the single-modality trap

Three categories of device claim to manage procedural pain. Each solves one axis and fails the rest. None of them close the loop between what a patient sees and what a patient feels, at any age.

The superficial buzzers

Competitors bHaptics · Woojer · CuteCircuit SoundShirt · TeslaSuit

The flaw 100% vibrotactile. Rigid mechanical buzzing that can't produce deep-tissue pressure or somatic pain gating, and adds its own mechanical hum.

The cognitive distractors

Competitors Stanford CHARIOT · Piñatas · Buzzy Bee

The flaw Passive visual distraction. Good at capturing attention, but it never intercepts the slow, sharp pain signal already climbing the spinal cord.

The passive regulators

Competitors Consumer TENS units

The flaw Zero visuo-haptic synchrony. No spatial resolution, dynamic movement, or ultra-low latency: the three things a neurological illusion requires.

None of these legacy approaches achieve synchronized, dual-haptic embodiment.

Two signals. One nervous system. No gap.

The objective: mitigate procedure-related needle distress, acute procedural anxiety, and somatic muscle guarding across routine pediatric immunizations and adult self-injections alike, including GLP-1 regimens and insulin for Type 1 Diabetes. DreamSleeve runs two physiologic channels in lockstep under a sub-5-millisecond sync budget, turning a passive medical encounter into an active, embodied experience.

01

Gate the pain

A tuned TENS field recruits large-diameter sensory fibers to close the dorsal-horn gate (the body's own volume knob on sharp pain) before the nociceptive signal arrives.

02

Render the texture

Layered piezo actuators paint pressure, movement, and fine texture onto the skin with spatial resolution a vibration motor can't reach. A felt surface, not a buzz.

03

Bind them in time

A shared clock fires both channels in visuo-haptic synchrony. When sight, current, and texture agree within milliseconds, the brain commits to the illusion: embodiment.

The console that makes the invisible legible

Every circuit on the sleeve reports impedance, alignment, and lock state in real time. Clinicians and self-injecting patients see exactly what the skin is receiving, and a profile-matched current ceiling, pediatric or adult, keeps it safe by design.

Why this wins, and why now

The category of one

Buzzers, distractors, and TENS units each own a corner. DreamSleeve is the only platform closing the visuo-haptic loop: a defensible wedge, not a feature race.
